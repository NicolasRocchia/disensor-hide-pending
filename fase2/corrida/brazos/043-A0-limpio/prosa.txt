FAB-3 was the largest contributor, but not a majority of all wafer-fabrication downtime:

- FAB-3: 1,307 minutes — 49% of the 2,651-minute total
- Of the leading issue, wafer-stage vacuum instability, FAB-3 accounted for 1,196 of 1,486 minutes — 80%

So one line nearly accounted for most overall and clearly dominated the largest delay category.
